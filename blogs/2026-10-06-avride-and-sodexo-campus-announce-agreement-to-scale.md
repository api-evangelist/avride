Avride and Sodexo Campus Announce Agreement to Scale Autonomous Delivery Technology Across U.S. College Campuses AUSTIN, Texas and NORTH BETHESDA, Md. — October 6, 2026 — Avride, an autonomous mobility company, and Sodexo Campus announced an agreement to accelerate autonomous robot delivery across Sodexo Campus partner colleges and universities in the United States, connecting Avride’s proven delivery technology with Sodexo Campus’s national higher education footprint.
